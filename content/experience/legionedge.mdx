LegionEdge is the parent company behind Nokuva, Foltrac, Tavoc, and W0rktree — building the next generation of developer tools.

Every product under LegionEdge starts from the same premise: the tools developers use every day are broken, and nobody is fixing them properly. Nokuva tackles design. Foltrac tackles deployment. Tavoc tackles the IDE. W0rktree tackles version control. Different problems, same company, same obsession with getting developer tooling right.
